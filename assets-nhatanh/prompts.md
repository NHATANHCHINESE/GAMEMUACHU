# Tài nguyên hình ảnh NHATANH CHINESE

Công cụ: imagegen tích hợp (built-in), không dùng CLI/API bên ngoài. Logo do người dùng cung cấp, giữ nguyên thiết kế.

Các PNG gốc và WebP tối ưu nằm cùng thư mục. Ảnh đã được nhúng trực tiếp vào cả hai HTML để chạy độc lập.

## nen-thung-lung

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original softly blurred natural landscape background for a Chinese vocabulary learning game on both desktop and mobile. Create a fresh environment independent of any reference game screenshot: a calm sunlit fantasy valley with a turquoise lake, rounded distant mountains, soft green meadow hills, fluffy clouds and golden morning light. A few small flowers and bushes frame the far edges. Eye-level depth across the landscape, not an isometric map. Friendly polished illustrated cartoon adventure style, clean softly shaded shapes, harmonious mint, aqua, light sky blue, soft leafy green and warm golden highlights. Square high-resolution composition that can be cropped to wide desktop or narrow mobile. The central 65 percent must be open, quiet, low detail, so vocabulary cards can fall across it. A gentle intentional soft-focus / Gaussian blur appearance across all scenery, about 4 pixels at 1024px. It should remain an illustrated game landscape, never a photograph. No characters, no houses, no village, no road map, no text, no letters, no numbers, no interface, no border, no watermark.

## yingying

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent original character sprite. One adorable chibi panda study companion YingYing for a Chinese learning game. Full body in a friendly three-quarter front standing pose, huge round cream-white head, little black ears, cocoa-black eye patches, large sparkling expressive eyes, soft pink cheeks, tiny joyful smile, squat body with stubby paws and feet. Wear a bright orange-red neckerchief and a little golden medallion. Hold a small open jade-green book in one paw and wave with the other. Polished illustrated chibi game-mascot / sticker art: thick clean warm dark-brown contours, bright harmonious colors, smooth soft cel shading, expressive face, subtle glossy highlights, very cute 2.5-heads-tall proportions. Match a cheerful illustrated girl mascot in a cream and orange-red Chinese-inspired student outfit. No fur texture, no photorealism, not 3D, not pixel art. Center only one full character with generous transparent padding around ears, waving hand, book and feet. No background scene, no ground plane, no circle or backing shape, no text, no logo or watermark. Genuine transparent alpha background.

## hoc-vien

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent PNG character cutout for an original Chinese vocabulary learning game. Redraw one cheerful chibi girl student as the matching companion to a panda mascot. Full body, friendly three-quarter front pose, black hair in two small round buns with orange-red ribbons, cream and orange-red Chinese-inspired student outfit with golden trim, dark little shoes, rosy cheeks, a warm smile, large expressive dark eyes. Holding a small open cream and teal learning book, one hand greeting the player. Cute squat 2.5-heads-tall proportions and oversized head. Art direction: polished casual mobile tycoon game / sticker-like storybook sprite, thick clean warm dark-brown outlines with round joins, bright flat color shapes with simple soft cel shading, saturated orange-red and warm cream, friendly hand-drawn feel. Not a 3D render, not anime realism, not pixel art. Only one original character, full body including buns, hand, book and shoes inside the frame with generous transparent padding; centered, isolated. No ground plane, no scenery, no circular backing plate, no text, no logo or watermark. Genuinely transparent alpha background with crisp clean contours.
